Direction Study 4 of 4, Revised — Broadcast Cockpit × Recruiting Board
Best Argument

The gauge and the italic numeral turn out to be football's own visual language, not a borrowed one. Rivals and 247Sports already render a "Crystal Ball" as a percentage circle, and every broadcast scorebug runs bold italic condensed numerals — this treatment just makes that instinct systematic across the whole recruiting board.

What Kills It

The gloss still reads closer to a draft-day TV package than to a program you run for decades. It sells the excitement of one kid choosing a school; it says nothing about the institution he'd be joining, which is the part of the sim this screen has to hand off to.

Where It Lands

Keep the gauge, the numeral, and the yard-line divider — sport-native now, worth using on other screens too. Keep the panel gloss and the deep glow to this one board, where a five-star's decision is the moment the game is supposed to be exciting.
