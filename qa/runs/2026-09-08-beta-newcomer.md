# Simulated beta tester: strategy player new to college football

Date: 2026-09-08. Game: `http://127.0.0.1:5195/College-Legends/`.

**Verdict:** There is an appealing small-business growth loop here. Researching Owen, winning his signature, fulfilling his ambition, and turning a strong performance into a commercial opportunity made me want another turn. Six offseason advances with almost no decision, repeated requests despite recent support, and an unclear route to sustainable cash weakened that interest. I would return for one more season to try a second client and understand the finances; continued retention is uncertain.

## Method and exact coverage

This is an independent **AI persona simulation**, not real human testing or an AAA panel. Persona: enjoys strategy, tycoon, and mobile games; knows essentially nothing about college football or sports agents.

Direct child-agent browser access failed before the first screen: no available browser. The parent/root then operated a dedicated fresh save and relayed live visible UI text, choices, and outcomes, occasionally abridging repeated wrappers. **Hands were the parent's; choices, expectations, and engagement judgments were mine.** This supports content-comprehension and decision-path findings, but does not establish my direct experience of layout, scrolling, click accuracy, or reading speed. Broker round trips extended wall-clock time beyond the intended 8–12 minutes; this is not an 8–12-minute timing result.

I did not read source, design documents, prior reviews, engine state, browser storage, or modified saves. I did not use outside football knowledge to resolve gaps.

Completed: fresh 2027 preseason, scouting/signing Owen Hill, three paid campaigns, two-week Vision training, recurring client requests, all 12 regular-season weeks, six offseason weeks, and entry into 2028 preseason with Owen returning. Not tested: professional preparation, draft income, borrowing, hiring, a second client, or every navigation area.

Parent captured and emitted the final 2028 Agency screenshot in the main conversation: cash$67,856, prestige18, 2028 Preseason, Build careers/goal panel and office. **No screenshot artifact was persisted; there is no screenshot-file link.** I did not independently inspect the image or console; the parent relayed those observations. ## Three supported fun moments and what to preserve

1. **Research and contested signing:** affordable information narrows uncertainty; the rival asks for a real margin-versus-probability choice. Preserve potential ranges, clear costs, and odds.
2. **W2 payout and ambition completion:** one action pays client and agency, builds trust/prestige, and produces a referral. Preserve the explicit client/agency split and consequence summary.
3. **W7–10 progression:** a visible training result, strong performance, expiring commercial opportunity, and prestige unlock create a reason to take another turn. Preserve clear duration, fatigue, and milestone feedback.

## Three dull or frustrating stretches

1. **Returning-client offseason:** repeated taps and automatic expenses to reach the next playable season. News does not replace agency decisions.
2. **Repeated coaching appeals W2/W6/W10:** identical text despite recent support weakens the relationship simulation; capped trust makes rewards less useful.
3. **Scouting burden and financial uncertainty:** twelve similar cards with unfamiliar vocabulary precede the first commitment, and later success lacks a clear path to covering overhead. This does not prove the economy cannot work: I tested one client and skipped draft income.

## Jargon and unclear decisions

- **HB, FS, EDGE, QB, WR, D-I FCS, conference, snaps:** I could proceed using ability/seasons, but cannot explain the business reason to prefer a position. Help explained stars better than position choice.
- **NIL:** menus taught me it earns money for the athlete. Its letters were not explained in the UI text relayed to me.
- **18-point /20.5-point game:** a reward/offer threshold appears without an obvious connection to the early yards/TD recaps. I cannot anticipate it.
- **Prestige vs public profile:** initially similar reputation numbers; prospect help usefully connects player profile to sponsorship.
- **Default Enter draft with multiple eligible seasons:** return control arrives atW12; paid preparation commits the draft route. This conflicts with a newcomer's assumption that three seasons implies a long-term signing unless explained early.
- **Rival reserved cash:** unclear whether it is the rival's budget, player benefit, or a price to match. My own chance and costs were sufficient to act.

## Five prioritized changes

1. **Condense offseason for returning clients.** Offer advance to next meaningful event/next season with a combined expense preview and brief recap, preserving actual decisions. Evidence: six advances and$9k after my return decision with no new substantive choice.
2. **Make requests acknowledge recent support.** Mention active training, completed promises, and current needs; suppress or vary redundant appeals. Explain incremental value when trust is capped. Evidence: identical request during the Vision plan and again atW10.
3. **Show a practical next step and cash outlook after the first payout.** Explain overhead runway, seasonal expenses, additional-client opportunities, and return/draft income paths using the current agency. Evidence: capital fell$32,144 after a successful relationship season, and I could not tell whether this was expected investment or failure.
4. **Simplify the first shortlist for outsiders.** Offer a few contrasting starter prospects with plain reasons—strong now, longer runway, easier signing, commercial fit—while retaining the full list. Expand abbreviations on demand and surface the useful agent-role explanation before the first choice. Evidence: I chose Owen largely because75 and three seasons were the biggest understandable numbers.
5. **Explain season choices and performance scoring early.** At signing, state when a player can return, how eligibility works, and that preparation commits draft entry. Show the same individual performance score alongside recap stats and prestige targets. Evidence: default draft surprised my long-term plan, and18-point targets were not predictable from the first recaps.

## Return verdict and confidence

**Cautious yes to one more season:** retain Owen, try a second client, and inspect the financial plan. W8–10 earned that interest; W13–18 spent much of it. Helping a chosen player improve is the strongest attachment. Fictional schools and scores have not yet earned equal interest from this persona.

Confidence: **moderate** for relayed content comprehension, repeated requests, and this one-client path; **low** for visual usability, reading/click speed, real human retention, optimal economics, and draft/professional systems. A different roster or persona could produce a different experience.
