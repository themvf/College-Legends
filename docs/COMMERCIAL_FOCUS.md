# Commercial agency focus

September 8, 2026. Supersedes the football-skills-first loop described in the earlier mobile flow notes.

The player represents athletes and prepares them for commercial opportunities. The repeatable loop is recruit → optional brand preparation → sign a campaign → deliver → get paid → decide the offseason career move. Football performance remains simulated background context.

## Changes

- Matching an overlooked prospect's priority at the standard fee now starts at 62% interest rather than 90%. Fees, prestige, ability, school stature and relationships affect that number; there is no guaranteed first signing. Contested prospects still use the existing rival negotiation. After a failed first pitch, the player can advance and retry during recruiting rather than getting stuck without a client.
- Brand Studio replaces the football development screen. The legacy Development promise key now means Build my brand; unfinished ability ambitions in old saves become public-profile goals. Already completed history and prepaid old training remain intact. Combine preparation remains a separate pro-career activity.
- A stylist/photo portfolio costs $750, takes one week and three client hours, and improves local and merchandise offers. Social production costs $2,000, takes two weeks and six hours/week, and improves profile, media and merchandise offers. Press/interview work costs $3,500, takes three weeks and two hours/week, and improves profile and media offers. These are explicit game rules, not real service quotes.
- Each service can be completed once per college career. Benefits persist, apply to future quotes and do not rewrite signed guarantees. A completed project never increases football ability. Client time creates a stated fatigue cost. The comparison includes agency commission and still-locked profile/prestige requirements. Spending is optional and need not pay for itself on the next deal.
- New ordinary and breakout sponsor deals have no activation charge. Sponsors fund campaign production; the agency earns its negotiated share on delivery. Existing saved deal costs are retained as historical spending.
- Booking, completion and payment are distinct feedback moments. Completion links directly to the selected client's updated offers. Preparation and campaign obligations cannot overlap for a client.
- Draft weekend automatically opens a client-by-client reveal, with pick and agency payout for selected players or an outreach path for undrafted players. Replaying the reveal never repeats a payment.

## Limits

This is a focused loop correction, not proof of enjoyment. Preparation benefits are deterministic and relatively simple; campaign production is abstracted into weekly delivery. There are still only three regular brand categories plus breakout offers. Broader economy balance, multi-season variety and the number of meaningful choices remain open design questions. Avoid adding more layers until this loop is understandable and worth repeating.
