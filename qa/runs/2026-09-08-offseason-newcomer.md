## Friction observed, including fix status

- **Initial payment-year wording confused me.** “Draft weekend” next to2029 made me wonder whether the commission would arrive an entire season later. The later UI receipt explicitly separated2028payment from2029school start. **Revised receipt was observed through the broker.**
- **An old recap contradicted the signed transfer.** Root relayed a statement about returning to Iron Valley Year5 after the Mountain Tech agreement, while payout/handoff said Mountain Tech. This could undermine trust in the commitment. Root reported a fix for newly generated recaps, but **this child did not observe a fresh replay of that corrected recap**. Final2029Mountain Tech handoff was observed.
- **After the one counter, disabled controls still displayed a hypothetical next ask of$230k.** That suggests another negotiation despite the stated limit. Root reported changing the text; **the revised disabled-counter text was not explicitly relayed**, so this report does not independently verify that fix.
- **$24k draft research is a large understanding cost.** I cannot say it is mispriced because I did not inspect the package or draft payouts. But on this save it made a basic unfamiliar path feel too expensive to investigate.
- **Renewal add-on at capped trust has little appeal.** At98trust, spending$2k for+8trust clearly loses much of its stated benefit. The free option was easy; this is a request for context, not a blocker.

## Prioritized suggestions

1. **Verify every recap respects the signed school and settlement status.** The final card was correct, but conflicting recap copy is more damaging than cosmetic wording. Replay the corrected generation in-browser before treating that observed contradiction as fully resolved.
2. **Preserve explicit payment season versus school season everywhere.** The observed revised receipt is the right pattern. Use it in confirmation, paid receipt, and handoff; keep “already paid” and “new sponsors separate.”
3. **Replace spent negotiation controls with a plain result.** Show counter used/accepted or rejected and current binding terms rather than a disabled hypothetical offer. Reported fix still needs visible verification on this evidence.
4. **Give a free plain-language draft baseline before expensive research.** Explain what a draft route could mean for client/agency income and what$24k research reveals; retain uncertainty rather than providing a free exact forecast. This would let a newcomer evaluate whether the research is worth buying.
5. **Make renewal extras show effective benefits at current trust.** At98trust, distinguish nominal+8 from remaining headroom, or explain any other applicable benefit. Keep free same-terms renewal and its clear confirmation.
