# Core player demo — September 7, 2026

The default playable now starts with scouting and recruitment. The previous enterprise prototype remains at `?enterprise=1`; the older single-player prototype remains at `?prototype=1`. This demo uses a separate browser save.

Recruit up to five named QB, HB, WR, FS and EDGE prospects. Supporting roster units have aggregate ratings. Scout uncertain upside; choose development support, environment, duration, intensity and a complementary practice partner. Media work increases recognition; technique work improves ability toward a hidden ceiling. Three concurrent project slots and one project per player constrain investment.

Sixteen fictional schools play twelve regular-season weeks. Each school faces twelve distinct opponents. Records and conference wins come from simulated games. Four schools enter actual semifinals and a championship. Rival players generate performances in those games; weekly offensive/defensive awards and six annual honors use those performances. The Founders Trophy is a fictional national player award. Draft ranks are explicitly projections among tracked eligible prospects, with 32 selections per round, not a complete professional draft.

Annual institutional player commitments, staff upgrades, unit investments, project costs, game receipts and bridge debt all post to the school cash ledger. Commercial campaigns have uncertain receipts; partner-paid athlete compensation is separate. A university bridge loan carries 10% annual interest. Negative cash ends the career; the season review requires $100,000 after debt repayment. Continuing seasons renew core-player commitments, graduate seniors and add prospects.

This is a playable systems demo, not the final economic balance or visual standard. Conference media negotiations, transfer negotiation, advanced mental-health stories, complete professional draft classes, position-specific box scores, animated campus characters and native iOS packaging remain future work. Existing original campus and portrait assets are reused; the interface is new. No claims of an App Store build or public deployment are made.
