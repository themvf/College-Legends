# Product Vision

## Status

College Legends is an original college-football business simulation in active early development. This document defines the committed product direction; individual mechanics remain subject to testing and balance changes.

## Product thesis

College Legends is a management game in which the institution—not merely the team—is the product.

The player inherits a financially vulnerable fictional football program and must create both competitive success and a durable organization. Recruiting and winning matter, but irresponsible growth can still destroy the program.

## Player fantasy

Begin as a hands-on operator with limited cash, weak facilities, incomplete information, and almost no margin for error. Over decades, grow into an executive leading coaches, departments, facilities, media properties, retail operations, and major events.

The intended emotional progression is:

1. Survive.
2. Establish a football identity.
3. Build a repeatable talent pipeline.
4. Turn competitive success into sustainable revenue.
5. Delegate through capable leaders.
6. Build an institution and legacy that outlast any one season.

## Core loop

Inspect information → allocate scarce resources → advance time → observe uncertain results → earn or lose money → adapt and reinvest.

The player should repeatedly face choices with competing athletic, financial, and organizational consequences.

## Design principles

### Decisions drive outcomes

The target is roughly 70% traceable decisions, 20% calculated uncertainty, and 10% genuine surprises. Chance should create tension and stories without routinely erasing strong preparation.

### Information is imperfect

Scouts, coaches, executives, and departments produce estimates rather than truth. Better people narrow uncertainty; they do not reveal the future.

### Fit beats isolated ratings

Players, schemes, teammates, coaches, development plans, and organizational resources interact. A highly rated person can fail in the wrong environment, while an overlooked prospect can become a legend in the right one.

### Success creates new problems

Growth brings debt, expectations, payroll, legal exposure, politics, operational complexity, and larger downside. Becoming powerful should change the problems, not end the strategy.

### Systems create stories

Narrative should emerge mainly from interacting systems: the star who transfers before a merchandise launch, the backup who flourishes under a new coach, or the expansion that wins games while destroying cash flow.

## Audience and platforms

The first playable release will be a private, desktop-primary responsive web alpha. Tablets and phones should support meaningful workflows, but phone constraints must not thin out the management depth.

The long-term target includes iOS. The simulation and domain model must remain platform-neutral so the web game can later be packaged or paired with native storage and services.

## Product boundaries

College Legends is not:

- A play-by-play action football game
- A real-world licensing project
- A thin roster spreadsheet with random scores
- A game where winning automatically fixes finances
- A simulation that hides arbitrary advantages behind AI difficulty

## Success criteria

The concept is working when testers can explain why their program improved or declined, form attachments to players and eras, face meaningful roster and financial pressure, and want to begin another season after seeing the consequences of their decisions.
